# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, assume
good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, respect privacy and intellectual property,
and never post another person's confidential CAD data or identifying information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project
spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
